Shopping list:

  • Bread
  • Butter
  • Milk
  • Egg
  • Lemon
  • Tea

Cooking plan:

  1. Tea:
    1. Heat some water
    2. Add tea bag and some sugar
    3. Add lemon (optional)
  2. Toast:
    1. Grill two pieces of bread
    2. Add butter
  3. Eggs:
    1. Mix milk with eggs
    2. Put the mix in a pan
    3. Fry the eggs
    4. Season with salt and pepper
    5. Remove the eggs when done
